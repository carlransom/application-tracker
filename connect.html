Connect your AI agents

Your AI assistant can read your pipeline, add applications, and answer questions about your search. It connects through MCP — one link, your Google sign-in, and only your data.

Step 1 — Copy the link

This is the address your assistant connects to. Copy it exactly:

https://application-tracker-mcp-web.azurewebsites.net/mcp

Step 2 — Add it in your assistant

Claude

  1. Open Claude and go to Settings → Connectors.
  2. Choose Add custom connector.
  3. Paste the link above and save it.
  4. Sign in with Google when asked — use the same Google account as your tracker.

Works on web, desktop, and phone. The Free plan allows one custom connector.

ChatGPT

  1. You need a paid plan (Plus or higher), on the web — this does not work in the phone app.
  2. Turn on Developer Mode, then go to Plugins.
  3. Create a custom MCP connector and paste the link above.
  4. Sign in with Google when asked — use the same Google account as your tracker.

Cursor

  1. Open Cursor and go to Settings → MCP.
  2. Choose Add new global MCP server and pick the URL type.
  3. Paste the link above and save it.
  4. Sign in with Google when the browser opens — use the same Google account as your tracker.

Step 3 — Ask it something

Try one of these:

  • “What applications do I have in interview right now?”
  • “Add an application: Operations Supervisor at Acme Logistics, Tampa, applied today.”
  • “Move my Ryder application to Interview.”
  • “How many applications have I submitted this month?”

What it can and can't do

  • Can: list your applications, search them, open one, show your stats, add a new application, update a status, and delete one you ask it to remove.
  • Can't: see anyone else's applications. Every person signs in with their own Google account, and the server only ever shows each person their own data.
  • Can't: do anything without your sign-in. No password is shared with the assistant — Google handles the sign-in.

Disconnecting

Remove the connector in the same place you added it (Claude: Settings → Connectors; ChatGPT: Plugins; Cursor: Settings → MCP). Removing it stops the assistant from reaching your tracker. Your applications stay in the tracker either way.
