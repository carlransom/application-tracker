FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes — sign up and start tracking. No card required.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your applications are tied to your account and visible only to you. The public demo uses separate sample data.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser, and you can add it to your home screen to use it like an app.

Is it really free?

Yes — free to use, no card required, and there is no paid plan today.

Who made this?

Application Tracker was built by Carl Ransom while job hunting — the tracker he wanted for his own search, made available for anyone.
