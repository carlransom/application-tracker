Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026
Operator / contact: Carl Ransom — carl.ransom@gmail.com

Application Tracker keeps your job search in one place. This policy explains what the app collects, what it does not collect, and how to delete your data.

What we collect

Account information (when you create an account):

  • Name and email address you enter at sign-up — or the name and email address from your Google account if you sign in with Google.
  • Password — handled by Firebase Authentication. The app does not store your password in plain text. (Google sign-in uses no password here at all.)

Application entries (only what you add):

  • Company, role, location, job type, status, date applied, closing date, pay, requirements, posting link, and notes you type in.
  • A server timestamp for when an entry is created.

Demo mode: the "Explore the demo account" option runs entirely in your browser. It uses sample applications and never sends data to Firebase. Nothing you do in demo mode is saved to an account.

What we do NOT collect

  • The app contains no advertising SDKs and no analytics code.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your application entries for advertising.
  • We do not read your email, contacts, or anything outside what you type into the tracker.

How your data is used

  • To let you sign in and see your own tracker on any device.
  • To save, sync, and display the application entries you add, edit, or delete.
  • To keep your account working (sign-in, password reset via Firebase Authentication).

Who processes your data

  • Google Firebase (Authentication + Cloud Firestore): stores your account and your application entries. Google processes this data under Google's terms and privacy policy. Your data is scoped to your user ID (users/{uid}/...) so one signed-in user only sees their own entries.
  • Google Sign-In: if you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address, and profile picture with Firebase Authentication to create your account.
  • Google Fonts: the app loads font files from Google Fonts when pages load. Google may see your IP address as part of serving those files, as with any site that uses Google Fonts.

No other third parties receive your data in the current build.

Security

  • Sign-in is required to see real (non-demo) data, and each user's entries are stored under their own user ID.
  • Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication, not stored by the app itself.
  • No system is perfectly secure. Use a strong, unique password and do not share your account.

Deleting your data

  • You can delete individual application entries inside the app at any time.
  • The app currently has no in-app "delete my account" button — it has sign-out only. Until one is added, request full deletion by emailing carl.ransom@gmail.com. Deletion means removing your Firebase Authentication account and your users/{uid} document and its application entries. We will confirm once it is done.

Retention

  • Your account and entries are kept until you delete them (entry-by-entry in the app, or full deletion on request as above).
  • Demo-mode data is never stored and disappears when you leave demo mode.

Children

The service is intended for job seekers managing their own applications. If you are under 18, use it only with a parent or guardian's permission.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted with a new effective date. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: carl.ransom@gmail.com.
